Recently, whenever I logged in over SSH, the CPU load averages were always above 2. A look at top showed them well into the tens…
Most of the resource-hungry processes belonged to the postfix user, so it was clearly the culprit.
I checked in webmin and found over ten thousand spam messages in Postfix's mail queue… That was where all the resources were going…
That gave me quite a scare… I thought my account had been compromised… But then I discovered that my SMTP server wasn't using authentication at all…
Here's an SMTP authentication testing site, for future reference…

http://www.antispam-ufrj.pads.ufrj.br/test-relay.html

Just search Baidu for SMTP authentication and such… There are plenty of tutorials…
I guess I can't afford to cut corners on security next time. I wonder whether mail providers will end up blocking this whole IP range…
