To Shooter (射手网), to YYeTs (人人影视), to the heroes' departure, and to past glory.
When the Nazis came for the Communists,
I remained silent;
after all, I was not a Communist.
When they imprisoned the Social Democrats,
I remained silent;
after all, I was not a Social Democrat.
When they came for the trade unionists,
I did not protest;
after all, I was not a trade unionist.
When they came for the Jews,
I remained silent;
after all, I was not a Jew.
When they came for me,
there was no one left who could protest.
—Martin Niemöller
